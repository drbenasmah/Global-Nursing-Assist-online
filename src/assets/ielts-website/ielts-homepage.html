Why Nurses Must Pass IELTS

International nursing regulators require proof of English proficiency to ensure safe communication, accurate documentation, and professional practice.

IELTS Academic is the most widely accepted English exam for internationally educated nurses.

Achieving Band 7 or higher is essential for registration in many countries.
